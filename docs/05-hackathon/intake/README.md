## Facts from the participant guide (read 21 November 2026)

- Venue: Bydgoskie Centrum Targowo-Wystawiennicze, ul. Gdańska 187. Mentors: "Strefa Mentorów" and Discord; the map is an image on the guide page (not readable by an agent: the owner looks at it).
- Saturday 20:00: draft in HackTribe; afterwards no new project and no change of task. Sunday 11:00: final submission; first judging stage starts. 14:45 finalists announced; 15:15 finalists present; 17:15 winners.
- First stage: jurors score the submissions against the task's criteria in a few minutes per project; the five best per task go to the final (the city's own regulations say at most three teams present). Scores of the first stage do not carry over.
- A team may enter two tasks with one project each. Our earlier research (`research/01`, verified on 20 November) adds: the second project needs a different team leader. For a single participant this means **one task**.
