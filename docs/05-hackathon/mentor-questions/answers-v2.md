# Mentor questions, version 2: what we answered ourselves and what is left to ask

Written 21 November 2026 (event day), after the five research files `research/14` to `research/18`. It replaces `questions-v1.md` as the list the owner takes to the partner's stand.

Result in numbers: version 1 had 46 questions (T1 to T21, B1 to B25). Research and the forensic facts settle 24 outright (some by evidence, some by a design that makes the answer irrelevant); 17 are partly settled and survive only as a short validation of our choice; 5 are context for the pitch and leave the tap list (B2, B3, B22, B24, B25). **Sixteen questions remain: 8 technical, 8 business.** Two of the technical ones are new: they come from `docs/03-plan/DECISIONS-forms-track.md` section 6 (J1 and J13).

How to read it:

- Evidence names: `forensics` = `reference-json-forensics.txt` (facts measured on the partner's two exports); `card` = `docs/05-hackathon/intake/coi-task-card.md`; `format` = `docs/02-architecture/coi-json-format.md`; `R14` to `R18` = the research files; `D1` to `D14` and `J1` to `J13` = decisions and open points in `DECISIONS-forms-track.md`. Where this file and DECISIONS disagree, DECISIONS wins until the lead changes it; the disagreements are listed in part 5.
- Confidence: high = measured or stated in the partner's own text; medium = inference from evidence; low = our default, no evidence.
- Polish text is what the owner reads aloud or shows; every remaining question can be answered with one tap. If the questions page offers a free note (typed or dictated), the open items B24 and B25 belong there, not in the tap list.

## 5. Contradictions and unverified points the lead must keep in mind

Contradictions between files, with the answer we picked:

1. **`.doc` is a mandatory input, and no file agrees on how to read it.** R14: LibreOffice behind a build switch, HTTP 415 without it, Tika as the alternative. R15: no permissive reader exists, LibreOffice is the realistic route. R16: an `olefile`-based text extraction with reduced fidelity. DECISIONS: our own reader in `core`, answer 422 as the fallback. Picked: our own reader, because a refusal of `.doc` fails the card's minimum on the first `.doc` at the defence. It is not built or tested yet, and the licence of `olefile` (BSD, from memory) was not retrieved in the research. Test it on the two reference `.doc` files first; keep Tika as the proven Apache-2.0 fallback.
2. **Id version.** Both exports carry only UUID version 4 (142 of 142). D10 says `uuid5`; R16 proposes a hash written in version-4 form; R16 derives ids from the hash of the input file (they change with any byte), D10 from meaning (stable across versions and formats). Picked: derive from meaning (D10). Open detail for the lead: writing the result in version-4 form matches the exports exactly and costs one line. `data/coi/README.md` notes that the second export has link ids that are not UUIDs at all, so their importer is probably lenient.
3. **Validators.** D8 writes them; both exports have none; R14 gives low confidence, R17 medium (KPA art. 63 par. 2 makes identity, address and the request mandatory content). R17 lists "does the generator accept validator names" as unknown, but the partner's own field description lists nine names (format, section 3), so the names are known and only their acceptance in a real import is untested. Question 3 of the technical list; the switch exists.
4. **Local model.** R18 says "not answerable from outside", R14, R15 and R16 read the card as allowing it. Moot while D12 holds.
5. **JWKS from a URL.** R14 offers it, R16 keeps it off by default. Picked R16.
6. **Alpine wheels.** R15 did not check musl wheels; R14 did, from PyPI file lists: the core set is covered; `onnxruntime`, `opencv-python-headless`, `pyclipper`, `morfeusz2`, `torch` are not. Nothing was installed in any research: the first `docker build --target core` with binary-only installs is the real test. Whether the `tesserocr` wheel bundles the engine is unverified: call the `tesseract` program from the Alpine package.
7. **Register sources in the JSON.** R17 would write `CEIDG.nip` as a default value; R15 warns that an unknown attribute may import and then fail silently. Picked: sidecar only, until business question 3 is answered.
8. **Check digits.** R14 and DECISIONS list KRS, land register and e-Delivery address among "our own check digits".
