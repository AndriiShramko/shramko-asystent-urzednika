Answers requirement R40. The first judging stage reads the submission **without the presenter** and spends a few minutes on it ([`research/01`](../../../research/01-hacknation-2026-task-rules-jury.md)). The live deck ([`storyboard-formularze.md`](storyboard-formularze.md)) has at most six words per slide and needs a voice; this version carries its own text and infographics. It is one source with three outputs:

All three are generated by `python tools/build_pakiet.py` (texts, drawings and the flag `BUILT` live in that one file). The idea behind the pages: [`../../01-product/one-visit-package.md`](../../01-product/one-visit-package.md).

## Rules of this version

- Every page: one headline, one drawing, at most about sixty words of body text, the source of every number in small type.
- Only true claims. While `BUILT = False` the first page carries the ribbon „Wersja robocza: opis rozwiązania budowanego podczas hackathonu" and page 9 says „W prototypie budujemy" instead of „Co działa". The lead sets `BUILT = True` only when page 9 equals `site-formularze/deck/status.json` and the truth table in [`../jury-qa.md`](../jury-qa.md).
- Numbers from the demo (`METRICS` in the generator) are hidden while they are `None`. Numbers inside drawings on pages 6 and 8 are examples and are labelled „przykład".
- No claim listed in one-visit-package §8. In particular: mObywatel and the city's booking calendar appear only as "docelowo" or "propozycja".
- Words to keep consistent: „mieszkaniec" (not „interesant" in running text), „druk" for the paper or file form, „komplet wniosków", „kontrola wyjątków", „na oryginalnym wzorze".

### 7. Data once

- **Headline:** Dane raz, z telefonu mieszkańca
- **Drawing:** a phone with a locked profile; two ways in: „1 dotknięcie: dane z przeglądarki", „aparat: dokument"; the office's QR code; only some fields fly to the office; a small clock. A side box „Docelowo: mObywatel".
- **Text:** Profil powstaje raz: przeglądarka podpowiada zapisane imię, adres i telefon, a dokument można odczytać aparatem. Profil zostaje na telefonie. Przy okienku mieszkaniec skanuje kod QR, widzi, które pola są potrzebne w tej sprawie, i zatwierdza. Dane trafiają do urzędu zaszyfrowane; dane tymczasowe znikają po wydruku, a w urzędzie zostaje podpisany wniosek.

- **RU:** Профиль создаётся один раз: браузер подсказывает сохранённые имя, адрес и телефон, документ можно считать камерой. Профиль остаётся в телефоне. ## Technical notes

- `tools/build_pakiet.py` writes `index.html`, `ru.html` (plain HTML, CSS and inline SVG, no network, no build step for readers). Edit texts, `BUILT`, `METRICS`, `WORKS`, `SIMULATED` at the top of the script and run it again.
- PDF: `google-chrome --headless --no-pdf-header-footer --print-to-pdf=docs/05-hackathon/presentation/pakiet/Od-potrzeby-do-podpisu.pdf docs/05-hackathon/presentation/pakiet/index.html` (each page prints as one 16:9 sheet; motion is off in print). Check the size (limit 10 MB) and the page count against the task card.
- Publishing: copy `index.html` to `site-formularze/pakiet/index.html`, add a card on `site-formularze/index.html`, push; the server pulls within two minutes ([`../../04-orchestration/deploy.md`](../../04-orchestration/deploy.md)).
- Before the final submission: open the PDF and read every page once against the software; a page that shows something not built is removed, not defended.
