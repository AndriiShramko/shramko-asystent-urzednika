# Demo script for the forms product: visitor and clerk

The owner's instruction: show a working product, not theory. This script is the product's acceptance test as much as its demo. It has two parts that map to slides 3 and 4 of the second deck ([`presentation/storyboard-formularze.md`](presentation/storyboard-formularze.md)). All data is synthetic.

## Who plays what

| Role | Device | Played by |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor | a phone (the owner's, or a juror's own phone if the network allows) | the owner or a juror |
| Clerk | the laptop, clerk's view | the owner |
| Analyst who digitises a form | the laptop, onboarding view | the owner |

Two browser windows on the laptop are enough if no phone can reach it: a narrow window plays the phone.

## Synthetic people and forms

- **Anna Przykładowa** and her child **Jan Przykładowy**, generated by the test-data tool (valid check digits, invented street in Bydgoszcz). Never type a real person's data, not even your own, on stage.
- **Form 1, digitised live in part one:** a short one-page form of the city hall that the audience has not seen in the product before. First choice: the city's template of a request for public information (it also joins the two tasks). Keep two more blank forms ready in case a juror picks one.
- **Form 2, used in part two:** the residence-registration form, the ministry's template with one character per box. Its descriptor is prepared and tested in advance (page 1 was measured before the event; the rest is measured during the build).
- **Bundle for part two:** "parent registers with a child": one data entry, two filled forms.

## Part one: a new form becomes a service (about 60 seconds)

| Step | Action on screen | What the audience sees | Line (Polish in the presenter window) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Nowy formularz", drop the PDF downloaded from the city's site | the page of the form | I upload a form from the office's site |
| 2 | "Rozpoznaj" | boxes appear on the fields; a list with proposed labels, types and links to the resident profile; a counter "fields found" | AI proposes the fields and what they mean |
| 3 | Correct one label and drag one box | the edit is instant | I fix what needs fixing |
| 4 | "Wydruk próbny" | a sample print with synthetic data over the original page; a green mark "identical outside the fields" | the print is checked against the original automatically |
| 5 | "Opublikuj" | the form appears in the list of services with a QR code; the generated phone form is shown | the online form is ready |

Numbers shown come from the run itself: seconds from upload to publication, share of fields proposed correctly. Say them only if they are on screen.

If recognition fails live: say so, open the form digitised earlier in the same session, continue. Rehearse this once.

## Part two: visitor and clerk (about 90 seconds)

| Step | Who | Action | What the audience sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clerk | "Nowy interesant" | a QR code and a short comparison code on the clerk's screen |
| 2 | Visitor | scans the code | the phone page: the office's name, the list of data that will be asked, language switch (PL, UK, EN) |
| 3 | Visitor | types the data, one field per row, or unlocks a saved profile with a PIN; adds the child, whose surname and address are pre-filled from the parent | fast entry; the counter of fields left |
| 4 | Visitor | "Pokaż, co wyślę", then "Wyślij" | exactly the fields that will be shared; then the comparison code |
| 5 | Clerk | the visitor appears in the waiting list; the clerk checks that the codes match and opens the record | the data in very large type, each field marked "podane przez interesanta" |
| 6 | Clerk | the checker's notes | one amber note, for example the birth date does not match the identifier, with a proposed fix; the clerk asks, corrects, confirms |
| 7 | Clerk | chooses the bundle "rodzic z dzieckiem" | two forms listed, one per person |
| 8 | Clerk | "Podgląd wydruku" | the filled form on the original template; the person's name in large type; "Drukuj" |
| 9 | Clerk | prints or saves the PDF | the paper with a dotted signature line; on screen: "dane usunięte" and the session countdown gone |
| 10 | Both | the phone shows "Dziękujemy" and "Usuń moje dane z telefonu" | nothing personal remains on the office side |

The strongest version: a juror scans the code with their own phone and types invented data. Offer it. If the venue network blocks phones, use the narrow window and say why.

What to say about speed: only what the screen measured (seconds from scan to print). Do not quote a multiple from memory.

## What each step proves to the jury

| Criterion they are likely to score | Shown by |
|---|---|
| Fit: a shorter path from a need to an online service | part one, steps 1–5 |
| Deployment potential | a real form of a real office, the original template, no change in the office's systems; the open registry |
| Validation and data security | step 6 (checker), steps 4–5 (only needed fields, comparison code), step 9 (deletion); "what the relay sees" view with ciphertext |
| Ergonomics | one field per row on the phone, large-type check, sticky print buttons, three languages |
| Innovation | AI onboarding with a human in the loop; the handoff prepared for the government wallet (shown as simulated) |
