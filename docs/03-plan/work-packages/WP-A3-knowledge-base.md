# WP-A3 — Knowledge base and search with sources

Kind: by spec · Model tier: middle, medium effort · Depends on: WP-00 · Branch: `wp/A3-knowledge` · Milestone: M1

## Goal

Everything the assistant is allowed to rely on, stored locally with its source address and date, searchable, and addressable down to a single provision of the act.

## Read first

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `research/02-udip-law-process-taxonomy.md`: section 1 (the acts and their current texts) and section 6 (machine-readable sources and working sample requests)
3. `docs/02-architecture/design-notes.md` §3 points 5 and 7

## Build

1. `knowledge/ingest_law.py`: fetch from the Sejm ELI API the current text of the access-to-public-information act and the provisions of the administrative procedure code and other acts that the research lists as relevant; split into units (article, paragraph, point) with canonical ids such as `udip:art.13:ust.2`; store text, act reference, the address and the fetch date. Save the fetched snapshot under `data/kb/law/` (public legal text) so the demo works offline; record the consolidated-text reference and the amendment the research names.
2. `knowledge/ingest_bulletin.py`: snapshot of public pages of the city's public information bulletin that answer frequent questions (start from the pages listed in the research and in `data/forms-catalog.json` under `bip_pages`); polite fetching, plain text plus address and date; store under `data/kb/bulletin/`.
3. `knowledge/ingest_archive.py`: load a **synthetic** archive of earlier requests and answers generated by `tools/testdata` (at least 60 pairs, some near-duplicates and one mass campaign) into the same store with `source = archive`.
4. Search: full-text search of the database with Polish-aware normalisation (lower-casing, diacritics-insensitive matching, simple stemming or lemma dictionary if available); optional embedding re-ranking behind a setting, off by default. `GET /kb/search` returns passages with `source`, `ref`, `address`, `as_of`, score. `GET /kb/provisions/{act}/{unit}` returns one unit.
5. `knowledge/citations.py`: `resolve(citation_string)` parses Polish citation forms ("art. 13 ust. 2 u.d.i.p.", "art. 16 ust. 1 ustawy o dostępie do informacji publicznej", "art. 107 § 1 k.p.a.") into canonical ids and reports unknown ones; `verify_quote(id, quoted_text)` checks a quotation against the stored unit, tolerant to whitespace.
6. A freshness note per source: when it was fetched and how to refresh (`python -m app.modules.knowledge.refresh`).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every article of the access-to-public-information act named in the research is retrievable by id; ten spot checks against the live API match the stored text.
- [ ] `resolve` handles at least 25 citation spellings from a test list and flags three invented ones (for example a non-existent article number).
- [ ] Search for five typical questions returns the expected provision or page in the top three (test list in the status file).
- [ ] All ingestion works offline from the committed snapshots; the network is needed only for refresh.
- [ ] `tools/gate.sh fast` passes.

## Out of scope

Case-law ingestion beyond the judgments the research cites by signature (store those as short notes with links); similarity of cases (WP-A8).
