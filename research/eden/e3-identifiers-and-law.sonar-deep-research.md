## Scope and verification status

**Position as at 21 November 2026.** “Verified” means supported by an official Polish source unless stated otherwise. **“Unverified” means that the detailed algorithm is widely implemented but I did not find it published in an official Polish source.** All examples below are synthetic test values that satisfy the stated syntax/checksum; they have **not** been queried against live registers and must not be treated as issued identifiers.

## 2. Applicant and representation model

### 2.1 Who should appear as the applicant

KPA Article 28 defines a party by its legal interest or obligation; Article 29 allows natural and legal persons and, in the stated cases, organisational units without legal personality. Legal/capacity questions are assessed under civil law, and non-natural-person parties act through statutory or charter representatives. [Current KPA text](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.Nsf/download.xsp/WDU20250001691/T/D20251691L.pdf) [404][405]

| Applicant type | Legal/form treatment | Recommended form branch and fields | Official source URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Natural person** | The person is the applicant. A person without capacity to perform legal acts acts through a statutory representative. | Name, surname, personal identification number or foreign identifier/document, address, contact details; separate “acts personally / through representative” choice. | [KPA arts. 28–30](https://isap.sejm.gov.pl/isap.Nsf/download.xsp/WDU20250001691/T/D20251691L.pdf). [404][595] |
| **Sole trader / CEIDG** | Not a separate legal person: the applicant remains the natural person conducting the business.
